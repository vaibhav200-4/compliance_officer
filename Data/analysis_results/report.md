# GDPR Compliance Report — Acme Corporation

## Executive Summary

GDPR Compliance Audit for 'Acme Corporation' evaluating document 'Global Privacy Policy'. The evaluation analyzed 99 article(s) spanning 149 requirement group(s) and 327 sub-obligation(s). The overall compliance score is 27.0% with an overall status of 'NOT_MET' and a risk posture of 'CRITICAL RISK'.

## Key Compliance Gaps

- **Group 5.1.quality_retention** (Article 5 — Accuracy and storage limitation): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy provides right to correction but lacks explicit 'prompt' timeframe. Retention periods are defined with legal exceptions but safeguards for extended retention are not explicitly stated.
- **Group 5.2** (Article 5 — Accountability): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy mentions data retention periods and legal bases for processing, which support accountability. However, there is no explicit evidence of records or documentation demonstrating compliance with Art. 5(1).
- **Group 6.3** (Article 6 — Legal-basis grounding for legal-obligation and public-task processing): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy lists 'Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws and regulations' as a legal basis, but it does not specifically identify the particular laws establishing the legal basis for processing other than mentioning Indian tax laws (Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST Act, 2017) in the context of retaining financial records.
- **Group 7.3** (Article 7 — Right to withdraw consent): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions the right to withdraw consent, but contains no commitment or statement confirming that data subjects will be informed of this right prior to giving consent. The policy does not state that withdrawing consent is as easy as giving consent.
- **Group 9.1** (Article 9 — Prohibition on processing special category data): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The supplied policy evidence makes no mention of special category data or its default prohibition under Article 9.
- **Group 11.2** (Article 11 — Notification and limited rights applicability where identification is not possible): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy contains no provisions regarding informing a data subject if the controller is unable to identify them. The policy contains no statements explaining that rights under Articles 15 to 20 do not apply when the controller cannot identify the data subject, nor does it address providing additional information for identification purposes.
- **Group 12.2** (Article 12 — Facilitating exercise of data subject rights): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The provided policy evidence does not mention the specific limitation regarding refusing to act on requests unless the data subject cannot be identified under Article 11(2) situations.
- **Group 12.3** (Article 12 — Timeframe and format for responding to rights requests): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy does not contain any provisions regarding extending the response timeframe by two further months for complex or numerous requests. The policy does not mention notifying data subjects of any extension and its reasons within the first month. The policy states that access requests will be provided in a commonly used electronic format, but does not explicitly address that all electronic requests are answered electronically unless otherwise requested.
- **Group 12.4** (Article 12 — Notification when controller does not act on a request): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy contains no provisions regarding the procedure or commitment to inform data subjects within one month with reasons when a request will not be actioned. The policy does not inform data subjects of their right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority or seek a judicial remedy in the event of a refused request.
- **Group 12.6** (Article 12 — Additional identity verification): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy does not mention or describe a process for requesting additional information to confirm the identity of a data subject when there is reasonable doubt about their identity.
- **Group 12.1.a** (Article 12 — Clear and accessible form of information): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy does not contain any sections specifically addressed to children. While it states 'Our Services are not directed at children under 13', this is a general disclaimer rather than information provided to children. No child-appropriate language or simplified content is present for child-directed information.
- **Group 13.1.ab** (Article 13 — Controller and DPO identification): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy provides a Contact & Grievance Officer and Data Controller, but fails to provide the contact details of a Data Protection Officer (DPO).
- **Group 13.2.ab** (Article 13 — Storage period and core rights): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy covers access, correction/rectification, and erasure (account deletion), but fails to explicitly mention the rights to restriction of processing, objection to processing, and data portability.
- **Group 13.1.cdef** (Article 13 — Purpose, legal basis, recipients and transfers): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy outlines the general legal bases for processing and some purposes across different sections, but does not explicitly map specific legal bases directly to each distinct processing purpose. The policy discloses international transfers and the applicable safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses), but does not state the adequacy status or the means by which to obtain a copy of the safeguards.
- **Group 13.2.cd** (Article 13 — Consent withdrawal and complaint rights): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions general complaints regarding the privacy policy under Section 12, but it fails to inform data subjects of their statutory right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- **Group 13.2.ef** (Article 13 — Statutory/contractual necessity and automated decision-making): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy lists legal bases for processing personal data, but it does not explicitly state whether the provision of personal data is a statutory or contractual requirement, nor does it describe the possible consequences of a failure to provide such data.
- **Group 14.1.cdef** (Article 14 — Purpose, legal basis, data categories, recipients and transfers): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy discloses international transfers and mentions appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), but does not state the means to obtain a copy of them.
- **Group 14.2.fg** (Article 14 — Source of data and automated decision-making): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The available policy evidence does not fully demonstrate compliance.
- **Group 14.1.ab** (Article 14 — Controller and DPO identification): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The available policy evidence does not fully demonstrate compliance.
- **Group 14.2.cde** (Article 14 — Core rights, consent withdrawal, and complaint rights): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy mentions access, correction, and deletion rights but omits restriction, objection, and data portability rights.
- **Group 14.4** (Article 14 — Notice of further processing for a new purpose): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy does not explicitly state that data subjects will be notified prior to processing for a new purpose. While consent is mentioned for marketing, there is no commitment to provide prior information about new processing purposes as required by Article 14(2).
- **Group 14.3** (Article 14 — Timing of providing information): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy does not commit to providing information at the time of first communication with the data subject, only responding to requests within 14 days. No evidence in the policy addresses disclosure to another recipient or timing of information provision in such cases.
- **Group 15.2** (Article 15 — Information on international transfer safeguards): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: Although the policy mentions international transfers and safeguards in Section 10, it fails to state that access requests under Article 15 will disclose these applicable Article 46 safeguards to the data subject.
- **Group 15.3** (Article 15 — Provision of a copy of personal data): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The provided policy text does not mention whether any fees may be charged for further copies requested by the data subject. The provided policy text states that requests must be submitted to privacy@acme.io (an electronic channel), but does not explicitly specify that the response will be provided in a commonly used electronic form unless requested otherwise by the data subject.
- **Group 15.1.a-f** (Article 15 — Confirmation of processing and core access information): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy states that data subjects may request a copy of their personal data held by Acme, but it does not explicitly mention the requirement to confirm whether personal data is being processed. The policy evidence for the Right of Access does not mention that access responses include the purposes of processing. The policy evidence does not state that an access response includes the categories of personal data concerned. The policy evidence does not mention disclosing recipients or categories of recipients, including those in third countries, as part of an access response. The policy evidence does not state that an access response includes the storage period or criteria for determining it. The policy details separate rights for rectification, erasure, and objection, but does not state that an access response specifically informs the person of all these rights (rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection). The policy evidence does not state that an access response informs the data subject of the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- **Group 15.4** (Article 15 — Copy right does not adversely affect others' rights): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy mentions the Right of Access (Art. 15) and states that data subjects may request a copy of their personal data, but it does not contain any acknowledgment or provision that copy provision may be limited or redacted to protect the rights and freedoms of third parties.
- **Group 16.1** (Article 16 — Right to rectification and completion): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy allows data subjects to request correction of incomplete data, but does not explicitly mention completion via a supplementary statement as stated in the legal requirement.
- **Group 17.3** (Article 17 — Exceptions to the right to erasure): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy does not reference a freedom-of-expression exception to erasure. The policy references retention required by law regarding the right to erasure, but does not cover public-interest or official tasks. The policy does not reference a public health exception to erasure. The policy does not reference an archiving, research, or statistical purposes exception to erasure. The policy does not reference a legal-claims exception to erasure.
- **Group 19.1** (Article 19 — Notifying recipients of rectification, erasure, or restriction): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy makes no mention of communicating rectifications, erasures, or restrictions of personal data to recipients to whom the data have been disclosed. The policy does not contain any commitment to inform the data subject about those recipients upon request.
- **Group 20.2** (Article 20 — Direct transmission between controllers): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy provides for the Right to Data Portability by allowing data subjects to request their data in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV/JSON), but it does not address or offer the right to have personal data transmitted directly from one controller to another.
- **Group 20.1** (Article 20 — Right to receive and transmit data in portable format): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy provides for the right to data portability in a structured, machine-readable format, but does not explicitly mention the right or commitment to transmit those data to another controller without hindrance.
- **Group 20.4** (Article 20 — Portability right does not adversely affect others' rights): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions the right to data portability under Article 20, but fails to acknowledge or state that portability exports may be limited to protect the rights and freedoms of third parties.
- **Group 20.3** (Article 20 — Scope limitation relative to erasure and public-task processing): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy limits data portability to 'data provided by consent or contract only', but it does not explicitly note that portability does not apply to processing necessary for the performance of a public-interest task or official authority.
- **Group 21.5** (Article 21 — Automated means of objecting in information society services): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy requires data subjects to submit all rights requests, including the Right to Object, manually via email to privacy@acme.io, and states that requests submitted through other channels are not guaranteed, failing to provide automated technical means for objecting.
- **Group 21.4** (Article 21 — Prominent disclosure of the right to object): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions the right to object under Section 6 (Data Subject Rights), but does not commit to explicitly bringing this right to the attention of the data subject at the latest at the time of the first communication. The policy includes the right to object as a bullet point under general data subject rights in Section 6, but does not provide evidence that it is presented clearly and separately from other information as required by Article 21(4).
- **Group 21.6** (Article 21 — Objection to research and statistical processing): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy evidence discusses the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing, but does not contain any provisions regarding the right to object to processing for scientific/historical research or statistical purposes under Article 89(1) or the public-interest task exception.
- **Group 21.1** (Article 21 — General right to object to processing based on public interest/legitimate interest grounds): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: Policy confirms right to object but does not explicitly state that processing must cease unless compelling overriding grounds or legal claims apply. No evidence of required cessation mechanism.
- **Group 21.2-3** (Article 21 — Absolute right to object to direct marketing): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy does not specify that processing must stop entirely upon objection. Only mentions cessation upon consent withdrawal.
- **Group 25.2** (Article 25 — Data protection by default): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy states that privacy-protective settings are applied by default and additional data collection requires explicit opt-in, but it does not comprehensively cover all aspects of processing extent, storage period, and accessibility by default. The policy discusses data retention schedules and access controls, but does not explicitly indicate that default-minimisation specifically applies across all four listed dimensions (amount, extent of processing, storage period, and accessibility) as a unified default principle. While the policy mentions role-based access controls and privacy-protective settings, it lacks explicit confirmation that personal data is not made accessible by default to an indefinite number of natural persons without individual intervention.
- **Group 27.3-4** (Article 27 — Location and mandate of the representative): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy mentions a DPO as representative but does not specify the Member State of establishment. Contact details include an Indian phone number (+91), which is not an EU Member State. No explicit confirmation of EU-based location for data subjects.
- **Group 28.9-10** (Article 28 — Written form of processor contracts and processor liability as controller): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The provided policy evidence does not contain any acknowledgment or statement regarding a processor being considered a controller if it infringes the regulation by determining the purposes and means of processing.
- **Group 28.2** (Article 28 — Sub-processor engagement authorization): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: Policy requires DPAs for processors but does not explicitly state prior written authorization from the controller is required before engaging sub-processors. Policy does not mention notification of sub-processor changes to the controller or opportunity to object under general authorisation.
- **Group 30.1** (Article 30 — Controller's record of processing activities content): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy identifies Acme Technologies Pvt. Ltd. as the Data Controller, but does not provide complete contact details, nor does it detail a representative, joint controller, or DPO contact details within a record of processing activities. The policy mentions lawful bases for processing, but does not contain a formal record of processing activities listing specific purposes for each processing activity. The policy references categories of personal data collected depending on the relationship, but does not provide a complete record of processing activities describing categories of data subjects and personal data as required. The policy mentions that third-party processors are engaged, but does not contain a record listing categories of recipients to whom data have been or will be disclosed. The supplied policy text contains no information regarding international transfers of personal data to third countries or international organisations or documentation of suitable safeguards. The policy mentions response timeframes for data subject requests such as erasure, but does not provide envisaged time limits for the erasure of different categories of data as part of a record of processing activities. The policy describes technical and organisational security measures implemented by Acme, though it is not explicitly structured as a record of processing activities under Article 30.
- **Group 32.1** (Article 32 — Risk-appropriate technical and organisational security measures): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy does not contain any specific text or measures describing the ability to restore availability and access to personal data in a timely manner following an incident.
- **Group 32.2** (Article 32 — Risk assessment factors for security measures): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions implementing technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or disclosure, but it lacks evidence that the security measures are chosen or assessed based on an explicit assessment of the risks presented by the processing.
- **Group 33.3** (Article 33 — Content of breach notification to the supervisory authority): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions that regulatory notifications will be made, but does not state that the notification will describe the nature of the breach, categories, or approximate numbers of data subjects and records. While the policy identifies the DPO and contact details in another section, it does not commit to including DPO or contact details specifically within the regulatory breach notification content. The policy contains no requirement or commitment to describe the likely consequences of the personal data breach in the notification. The policy does not include a requirement or commitment to describe measures taken or proposed to address and mitigate the breach within the regulatory notification.
- **Group 33.4** (Article 33 — Phased provision of breach information): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy evidence does not mention or acknowledge that breach information may be provided in phases when it is not immediately available.
- **Group 33.5** (Article 33 — Documentation of personal data breaches): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions maintaining a breach log with details of each incident, actions taken, and outcomes, but does not explicitly detail that the documentation comprehensively covers all 'facts' and 'effects' as required by Article 33(5). The policy lacks any statement indicating that the breach documentation is maintained in a manner designed to enable supervisory authorities to verify compliance with Article 33.
- **Group 34.4** (Article 34 — Supervisory authority power to require communication): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy does not acknowledge that the supervisory authority may require individual breach notification or determine exemptions when individual notice was not given.
- **Group 34.1-2** (Article 34 — Timely, clear communication of high-risk breaches to data subjects): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy does not specify that communications include contact details, likely consequences, or mitigation measures as required by Article 34(2)(1).
- **Group 35.7** (Article 35 — Required content of a DPIA): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions that a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is conducted, but it does not specify that it includes a systematic description of processing operations and purposes including legitimate interests. The policy does not contain any evidence confirming that the PIA includes an assessment of the necessity and proportionality of the processing operations. The policy references high risk to individuals regarding the PIA trigger, but does not detail a comprehensive risk assessment of risks to rights and freedoms of data subjects. The policy outlines general data security measures, but does not state that the PIA includes the specific measures and safeguards envisaged to address identified risks.
- **Group 35.9-11** (Article 35 — Stakeholder input, exemption, and ongoing review of DPIAs): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions conducting a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) for high-risk processing activities, but does not indicate that the views of data subjects or their representatives are sought during this process. The policy contains no mention of exemptions from DPIAs based on prior general legislative impact assessments for processing pursuant to Article 6(1)(c) or (e). The policy requires a PIA for high-risk activities, but contains no commitment or requirement to review the PIA when the risk represented by processing operations changes.
- **Group 37.7** (Article 37 — Publication and communication of DPO contact details): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy states the DPO is the 'primary contact for supervisory authorities,' implying communication of details, but does not explicitly state that the contact details are formally communicated to the authority.
- **Group 38.1-2** (Article 38 — Timely involvement and support of the DPO): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy confirms the DPO has access to data (e.g., breach assessment) and receives support via training record reviews, but lacks explicit mention of resource allocation (e.g., budget, tools) required for full compliance.
- **Group 38.3** (Article 38 — DPO independence and protection): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy does not contain any explicit provisions protecting the DPO from dismissal or penalties for performing their tasks. No evidence supports this obligation.
- **Group 38.4-5** (Article 38 — Data subject access to the DPO and DPO confidentiality): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: Policy provides DPO contact (dpo@acme.io) but directs data subject requests to privacy@acme.io. DPO's role as primary contact for supervisory authorities is stated, but not explicitly for data subject rights. No evidence confirms the DPO is bound by secrecy or confidentiality obligations. Policy mentions DPO responsibilities but does not specify confidentiality requirements.
- **Group 39.1** (Article 39 — Minimum DPO tasks): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy notes that training records are reviewed by the DPO and that the DPO oversees the policy, but does not explicitly confirm comprehensive compliance monitoring, assignment of responsibilities, or audits of staff training as detailed in the requirement. The evidence mentions that a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) is conducted for processing activities, but does not state that the DPO provides advice on or monitors its performance. The policy mentions the DPO acts as a contact point for supervisory authorities, but does not explicitly mention cooperation with the supervisory authority. The policy states the DPO acts as the primary contact for supervisory authorities, but does not explicitly mention prior consultation or other consultation matters.
- **Group 44.1** (Article 44 — Overarching transfer compliance principle): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy specifies safeguards used for international data transfers (SCCs, adequacy decisions, BCRs), but does not explicitly mention onward transfers or state that all conditions of Chapter V are complied with. The policy mentions safeguards for international transfers such as SCCs, adequacy decisions, and BCRs, but it does not contain an explicit commitment ensuring that international transfers will not undermine or reduce the level of data protection guaranteed under the GDPR.
- **Group 45.1** (Article 45 — Transfers under an adequacy decision): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy identifies adequacy decisions as a safeguard for international data transfers, but it does not explicitly state that no additional transfer safeguard or authorisation is required for those specific transfers.
- **Group 46.1** (Article 46 — Core safeguard requirement absent an adequacy decision): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The policy mentions safeguards for international transfers such as Standard Contractual Clauses, but it does not explicitly confirm that enforceable data subject rights and effective legal remedies remain available to data subjects following such transfers.
- **Group 47.2** (Article 47 — Mandatory BCR content): Status `PARTIALLY_MET`. Gap: The provided policy evidence does not contain details regarding the group structure and the contact details of each member of the group of undertakings. The policy outlines data subject rights (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Data Portability, Object) and a designated contact channel, but does not explicitly confirm binding corporate rules' legal effect or apply general data protection principles in the context of BCRs. The provided policy evidence contains no mention of an EU-established controller or processor accepting liability for breaches of binding corporate rules by non-EU members. The provided evidence does not describe how binding corporate rules are communicated to data subjects beyond standard Articles 13 and 14 notices. While the DPO role is mentioned, the evidence lacks comprehensive details covering compliance monitoring tasks, complaint procedures, verification mechanisms, change reporting, supervisory authority cooperation, and reporting of conflicting foreign legal requirements specific to BCRs. The provided company policy evidence does not describe specific data transfers, categories of personal data, processing types/purposes, affected data subjects, or destination countries in the context of Binding Corporate Rules.
- **Group 77.1** (Article 77 — Disclosure of the right to complain to a supervisory authority): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy lists several data subject rights under Section 6 (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Data Portability, Object), but it completely fails to inform data subjects of their right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- **Group 79.1** (Article 79 — Disclosure of the right to a judicial remedy and applicable venue): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy does not mention the right of data subjects to an effective judicial remedy against the controller or processor where their GDPR rights have been infringed. The company policy contains no information regarding the jurisdiction or venue where judicial proceedings may be brought (courts of the Member State of establishment or habitual residence).
- **Group 80.1** (Article 80 — Right to be represented by a qualifying not-for-profit body): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The company policy addresses data subject rights under Sections 5 and 6 (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Portability, and Object), but completely fails to acknowledge or mention the data subject's right to mandate a not-for-profit body to lodge complaints or pursue remedies on their behalf.
- **Group 82.1** (Article 82 — Disclosure of the right to compensation): Status `NOT_MET`. Gap: The policy lists various data subject rights under Section 6 (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Portability, Object), but contains no mention of the right to compensation for material or non-material damage caused by a GDPR infringement pursuant to Article 82.
