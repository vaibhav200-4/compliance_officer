This document describes how TechStartup Pvt Ltd collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data of its
users. It applies to all products and services offered through www.techstartup.com and our mobile
applications.
1 Introduction & Scope
TechStartup Pvt Ltd ('we', 'our', 'us') is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy
applies to all individuals who access or use our platform, mobile applications, APIs, and related services
(collectively, the 'Services').
This policy is governed by applicable Indian data protection laws and, where relevant, the General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users based in the European Economic Area (EEA). We operate under
the Information Technology Act, 2000 and IT (Amendment) Act, 2008.
By using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do
not agree with any part of this policy, you must discontinue use of our Services.

3 Legal Basis for Processing
We process personal data only when we have a valid legal basis to do so. The legal bases we rely on are:
(cid:127) Contractual necessity — to provide the Services you have signed up for
(cid:127) Legitimate interests — for fraud prevention, security, and product improvement
(cid:127) Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws and regulations
(cid:127) Consent — for marketing communications and optional analytics (you can withdraw at any time)
Where we rely on consent, you have the right to withdraw it at any time by contacting us at
privacy@techstartup.com. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that
occurred before the withdrawal.
4 Data Retention
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy.
4.1 Active Accounts
Data associated with active accounts is retained for the duration of the account's active status and for a
period of 12 months after the account is closed, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
4.2 Inactive Accounts
Accounts that have shown no login activity for a period of 36 consecutive months are considered inactive.
We will send a notification email 30 days before automatic deletion. Inactive accounts and all associated
data are permanently deleted after the 36-month inactivity period.
4.3 Financial Records
Transaction records and invoices are retained for 7 years in compliance with Indian tax laws (Income Tax
Act, 1961 and GST Act, 2017). These records may be anonymized after the active account period but
must be retained in some form for the legally mandated period.
4.4 Support Data
Support tickets and communication logs are retained for 24 months from the date of the last interaction to
enable quality review and dispute resolution.
5 User Rights
As a user of our platform, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of
these rights, please contact privacy@techstartup.com with the subject line 'Data Rights Request'. We aim
to respond to all requests within 14 business days.
5.1 Right to Access
You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We will provide this in a commonly used
electronic format.
5.2 Right to Correction
If any personal data we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request that we correct or
update it. You may also update basic profile information directly in your account settings.
5.3 Account Deletion
Users who wish to close their account may do so by sending an email to privacy@techstartup.com. Upon
receiving a valid deletion request, we will process the request and respond within 14 days. Please note
that some data may be retained as required by law (see Section 4.3).
5.4 Marketing Opt-Out
You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by clicking the 'Unsubscribe' link in any marketing
email or by contacting privacy@techstartup.com.

7 Data Security
We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data
against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or alteration.
(cid:127) All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3
(cid:127) All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
(cid:127) Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only via MFA
(cid:127) We conduct quarterly internal security audits
(cid:127) Penetration testing is conducted annually by a third-party security firm
In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users via
email within a reasonable timeframe. We also notify relevant regulatory authorities as required by
applicable law.
8 Cookies
We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve user experience and analyse platform usage.
You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
Types of Cookies We Use
(cid:127) Essential cookies — required for the platform to function (cannot be disabled)
(cid:127) Analytics cookies — used by Mixpanel to understand how users interact with the platform
(cid:127) Preference cookies — to remember your language and display settings
We do not use advertising or targeting cookies. You may disable non-essential cookies at any time
through your browser settings without affecting core platform functionality.
9 International Transfers
Our primary servers are located in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) region. Some of our third-party service
providers (such as Mixpanel and Sentry) may process data outside India, including in the United States
and European Union.
When transferring data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard
Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where required under GDPR for EEA user data.
10 Children's Privacy
Our Services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact
privacy@techstartup.com immediately and we will delete it promptly.

When we make significant changes, we will notify users via email and display a prominent notice on the

The 'Effective Date' at the top of this document indicates when the policy was last updated. Continued use
of our Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
